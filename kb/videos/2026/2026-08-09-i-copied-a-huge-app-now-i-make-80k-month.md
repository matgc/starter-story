# I Copied A Huge App. Now I Make $80K/Month

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ)

## Marketing strategy summary
- Target a large, universal niche in an underserved local market: Tomer adapted a proven US AI calorie-tracking concept for Israeli fitness and nutrition users rather than inventing a new category.
- Localize beyond translation: the Hebrew app adjusted food language and eating patterns, while its local brand, design, and content created the main differentiation.
- Choose acquisition by local economics. Paid ads were most efficient because Israeli CPMs were significantly lower than US CPMs; influencers, SEO, and ads can all work, but channel efficiency varies by market.
- Build creatives by studying both viral US content from similar apps and viral Israeli fitness and nutrition content, then combining those patterns for ad campaigns.
- Use a staged distribution loop: paid ads produced traction, happy-user proof, and about $20K/month within 4 months; that evidence improved negotiating leverage with major influencers, whose involvement helped grow revenue from $20K to $80K/month.
- Replace one-off influencer fees with revenue sharing so incentives stay aligned. Contracts should define monthly content volume, filming or recording days, and timelines; Tomer's partners shared revenue from Israel and became recognizable faces of the brand.
- Monetization was subscription-based; revenue rose from about $1K in the first month after the June 2025 launch to over $81K in April 2026. Caveats: the influencer share was not disclosed, the founder had marketing experience and a personal connection in the niche, and he advises proving demand before approaching major talent.

### Reusable playbook
1. Find a proven app in a very large niche that is still poorly served in your country, language, or local market.
2. Adapt its core experience to local language, behavior, culture, brand, design, and content rather than making a literal clone.
3. Compare channel economics locally, then test creatives modeled on winning category content from both the original and target markets.
4. Use the most efficient channel to reach meaningful revenue and collect user satisfaction and social proof; Tomer's paid-ad phase reached about $20K/month in 4 months.
5. Approach major niche influencers only after traction, negotiate from that proof, and use a detailed revenue-share agreement to turn them into recurring distribution partners rather than one-off sponsors.
