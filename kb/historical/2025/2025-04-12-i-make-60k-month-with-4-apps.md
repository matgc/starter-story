# I Make $60K/Month with 4 Apps

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8)

## Marketing strategy summary

- Jure's portfolio matches different products to their natural buyers and channels: Rummer sells a funny, controversial fake-engine-sound device to car owners through social video; Parakeet AI serves job seekers with real-time interview help; Optibase reaches Webflow users through its app store, Google, and word of mouth; and Parakeet AI Apply Agent automates job applications.
- Rummer's idea came from overlapping firsthand activity: building Shopify stores showed him e-commerce economics, while buying a used car exposed him to a diagnostics device that could read live RPM data. He chose a cheap, impulse-friendly product whose result could be understood in a 10-second video.
- Validation starts with existing competition rather than assuming an idea is unique. His test is whether he can make the product meaningfully better or market it better, followed by a viable MVP that is good enough to earn a first sale; he says Parakeet AI went from proof of concept to product in about two weeks.
- Organic short-form video became Rummer's primary acquisition channel after Facebook and Google ads failed. He recommends ranking the available channels, committing fully to the most promising one for two months, and doubling down only on what produces results.
- The team turns a small set of components into many tests: it batches five to 10 ideas, combines multiple hooks, main sections, and endings, then repeatedly remakes the concepts that work. The product itself supplies the hook because fake engine sounds are visual, funny, and controversial.
- Jure reports that roughly 200 million organic TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube views generated about 600,000 site visits, more than 6,000 orders, and $300,000 in revenue over the last year. He found his content co-founder by emailing hundreds of creators, and their revenue split aligns incentives; the creator's second video reportedly went viral.
- Conversion stays simple at Rummer: traffic lands on a basic page, and the team favors large, high-confidence improvements over low-signal A/B tests. Monetization follows product usage: Parakeet AI sells one-time credit packs instead of a subscription because interviews are episodic, making "no subscription" a competitive sales message.
- The four businesses reportedly produce $60,000 per month, including more than $35,000 in Parakeet AI revenue over the prior 30 days and about $6,500 in Optibase MRR. Caveats: all results and organic attribution are self-reported, the figures cover different time periods, Apply Agent was only two weeks old, and profit, conversion by platform, retention, and customer acquisition labor are not disclosed.

### Reusable playbook

1. Look for ideas where two areas of firsthand experience overlap, then confirm that buyers already spend money on competing solutions.
2. Choose a clear advantage: build a materially better product or package the idea for a distribution channel competitors use poorly.
3. Match the offer to the channel; for short-form video, favor an inexpensive, low-consideration product with a surprising result viewers understand within seconds.
4. Build the smallest product good enough to earn a real sale, launch quickly, and let market response determine the next investment.
5. Rank a limited set of acquisition channels, run the strongest candidate with full focus for two months, and stop spreading effort across weak channels.
6. Batch interchangeable hooks, demonstrations, and endings into many videos, measure which concepts drive visits and orders, and remake winners with new variations.
7. Recruit a complementary creator through targeted outbound outreach and use performance-linked compensation when both sides accept the risk.
8. Keep the landing page focused on the core promise, prioritize large conversion improvements, and price according to actual usage rather than forcing every product into a subscription.
