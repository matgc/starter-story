# How I Work: $10M/Year Serial Entrepreneur

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ)

## Marketing strategy summary

- Choose a niche at the intersection of what the world wants, what people will pay for, what you are good at, and what you love doing; if you need momentum, start first and pivot from early results.
- From $0 to roughly $5M-$10M in revenue, prioritize getting more customers and improving the product rather than prolonged planning.
- Brute-force acquisition with a "zebra calendar": 15-minute customer meetings separated by 5-minute breaks, repeated throughout the day.
- In the early stage, personally build the website, write the copy, do the design work, and keep moving from the next concrete task to the next.
- Validate through quantity and iteration: produce many attempts, learn while doing, and improve from feedback instead of waiting for one perfect result.
- For content, research each guest, supplement that research with books or topic research, and publish podcasts consistently on Monday and Wednesday; avoid chasing views or downloads with ideas that undermine enthusiasm.
- Once the business reaches roughly $5M-$10M in revenue, hire and delegate; protect what is already compounding by experimenting in a separate sandbox instead of disrupting the core business.

### Reusable playbook

1. Define an offer around customer demand, willingness to pay, your strengths, and work you enjoy.
2. Schedule repeated 15-minute customer conversations with 5-minute gaps to uncover needs and win customers.
3. Ship the website, copy, design, or content yourself, then improve it through repeated attempts and customer feedback.
4. Focus each day on one or two specific, time-bound tasks that increase customers or improve the product.
5. At roughly $5M-$10M in revenue, delegate operations and test new ideas away from the compounding core.
