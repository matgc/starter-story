# I made $1M flipping apps

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI)

## Marketing strategy summary

- Build small, narrow products around concrete jobs such as generating Excel formulas, cover letters, summaries, quizzes, or landing pages, then set the intended sale horizon before development so the roadmap matches a 30-day, three-month, or longer exit goal.
- Use X as both the research and primary organic acquisition channel: watch trends and sentiment for timely product opportunities, publish a single landing page through a build-in-public account, and collect emails before building.
- Treat a roughly 15% landing-page conversion rate as Dom's validation benchmark, then build only one key feature and send it to the first email subscribers.
- Run a tight build-feedback-edit loop: ask that first user batch for feedback, improve the product, relaunch, and repeat rather than completing a broad feature set in isolation.
- Timing was the reported difference between stronger and weaker projects; the portfolio ranged from $300 in revenue and a $2,500 sale to about $7,000 in revenue and a roughly $25,000 sale, while other exits were reported at $5,000, $7,000, $12,000, and $34,000.
- Package each product as a transferable asset by tracking recurring revenue, gross margin, average revenue per user, lifetime value, customer acquisition cost, and churn, the metrics buyers use to judge whether someone else can operate it.
- Dom reports that Softgen grew from $20,000 to half a million in recurring revenue in three months and sold for nearly three times recurring revenue; the automatic captions do not clearly state the revenue period, and no independent verification or full acquisition economics are provided.

### Reusable playbook

1. Choose a specific exit horizon and a narrow, trend-supported customer task before defining the product roadmap.
2. Publish a one-page offer on X, build in public, collect emails, and require a meaningful landing-page conversion signal before coding.
3. Build one essential feature, give it to the first subscribers, and repeat a build-feedback-edit-relaunch loop.
4. Keep organic X distribution active while testing supporting outreach, influencer, UGC, Reddit, and paid-ad tools only where results can be measured.
5. Improve recurring revenue, margin, customer value, acquisition cost, and churn, then document the operation so a buyer can take it over.
6. Sell when the product reaches the goal set at the start, and reuse the same validation and execution system for the next small product.
