# Starter Story Transcripts

A reference archive of publicly available video transcripts from the [Starter Story YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@starterstory), covering videos published from January 1, 2026 onward.

The goal is to make proven app marketing and sales strategies easier to search, study, and use with AI tools.

## Planned contents

- One transcript per video
- Video metadata and source links
- Publication dates
- A searchable index
